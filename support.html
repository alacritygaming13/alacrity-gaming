Common Questions

How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?

Open STACK and go to Settings, then select Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account originally used to purchase Remove Ads.

I purchased Remove Ads but I'm still seeing an ad.

Remove Ads removes forced advertisements. Optional rewarded ads, such as watching an ad to continue a run, may still be available when you choose to use them.

How do I report a problem?

Email us and include a description of the issue and, if helpful, your iPhone model and iOS version.
