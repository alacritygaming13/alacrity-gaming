Privacy Policy

STACK: Tower Challenge

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Alacrity Gaming operates STACK: Tower Challenge. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you play STACK.

Information We Collect

STACK does not require you to create an account and does not directly request your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

The app may automatically collect information about app usage, gameplay interactions, device or app identifiers, approximate location, device information, performance information, and diagnostic information. The information collected depends on the services used and your privacy choices.

Analytics

STACK uses Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand how the game is used and improve the player experience. Analytics may include information such as app interactions, gameplay events, device and app information, and identifiers generated for analytics purposes.

Advertising

STACK uses Google AdMob to provide advertising. AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising data, app interactions, and diagnostic information depending on your region, privacy choices, and the advertising configuration used by the app.

Where required, STACK uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present privacy or consent choices before advertising is requested. Privacy choices may also be available again from within the app when required.

App Tracking Transparency

On Apple devices, STACK uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework where required. Before advertising activity that may involve tracking occurs, the app may ask for your permission to allow tracking.

If you select "Allow," advertising services may use permitted identifiers and advertising data in accordance with your other applicable privacy and consent choices.

If you select "Ask App Not to Track," STACK remains fully functional. Advertising may still be shown where permitted, but the app does not authorize tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and advertising is configured to operate without tracking authorization.

Your App Tracking Transparency choice can also be managed through your device's iOS or iPadOS privacy settings.

Game Center

STACK may use Apple Game Center for features such as leaderboards. Information processed through Game Center is handled by Apple according to Apple's privacy practices.

In-App Purchases

STACK may offer an optional in-app purchase to remove certain advertisements. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Alacrity Gaming does not receive your payment card information.

Third-Party Services

STACK uses services provided by third parties, including:

  • Apple Game Center and App Store services
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Information processed through third-party services may be retained according to the policies and settings of those providers. Locally stored game information, such as settings, progress, scores, Stars, and unlocked content, may remain on your device unless it is removed through normal app or device functionality.

Children's Privacy

STACK is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may be provided with choices regarding advertising and data processing. Where required, these choices can be accessed through the Privacy Choices option available within STACK.

On Apple devices, you may also receive an App Tracking Transparency permission request. Declining tracking does not restrict gameplay or access to app features.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when STACK or the services it uses change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
